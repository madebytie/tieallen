## 0. Positioning thesis (draft — Tie to confirm/adjust)

The market position with the best evidence-to-demand ratio is NOT "senior product
designer with 18 years" (commodity lane, competes with laid-off big-tech designers
on logo strength). It's: **the one-person product org — a designer who ships
production AI products end to end, with live URLs as proof.**

### Target lanes, ranked

| # | Lane | Why | Flagship story |
|---|------|-----|----------------|
| 1 | **Founding Designer — seed→Series B AI startups** | They *want* the solo-builder profile; no one asks why you lack big-org history; fractional→full-time is a normal path here | Whichever product matches their space |
| 2 | **AI-native product builder/lead — AI product & devtools companies** | Stack overlap is literal; almost no other applicant shows MCP/agent orchestration shipped in production. **Route through founders/product leadership, never engineering hiring loops** (see identity rule below) | Scope Architect (in-category) |
| 3 | **Founder in Residence / venture studio EIR** | Four 0→1 platforms in 16 months is the exact credential; often part-time friendly | The 4-product portfolio itself |
| 4 | **AI Product Lead — mid-size cos retrofitting AI** | Real demand, but "Lead" implies people-management evidence we don't have; frame as IC who owns the AI surface | Vizon OS |
| 5 | **Principal/Staff PD — established companies** | Weakest lane: committee hiring + leveling rubrics punish nontraditional history. Keep the leadership resume alive for it, but don't spend outbound energy here | — |

### Identity rule (Tie, July 9): builder, NOT engineer — decided

Tie's own words: not a hard-core programmer/coder/engineer — he designs and
builds hard-core products end to end *with AI*, and does not want interviews
that require writing code or "traditional nonsense." What he's shipped should
speak for itself; the appeal is to founders and product teams.

Consequences (applied throughout this doc):
- **"Design Engineer" vocabulary is retired** — that title attracts coding
  screens and take-homes. Same for "full-stack development" claims on assets.
- The identity is: **designer who ships production software through
  AI-orchestrated development** — the proof-of-concept of the operating model
  every AI-native team is moving toward. That's *more* differentiated than
  "designer who codes," and it's honest.
- Outbound filter: skip roles whose process includes coding challenges. The
  counter-offer where there's mutual interest: "instead of a take-home, I'll
  walk you through a live production system I built and every decision in it."
- /hire already tells this story correctly ("I own the design, platform, and
  product direction, and AI handles the bulk of the implementation") — the
  resumes must match it. Current founding-variant summary says "front-end and
  full-stack development" → needs the reframe (§3.5).

### The two objections every asset must answer

1. **"Can he work inside a team?"** (the lone-wolf flag) — answered by: IPG entry,
   agency-years-as-embedded-teamwork framing, "Built Solo, Designed for Teams"
   pillar, and testimonials chosen specifically for *collaboration* evidence.
2. **"No users on the founder products."** — answered by: honest capability metrics
   (already the house rule), demo videos, and making the Vizon OS client teams
   referenceable if possible.

### Part-time / fractional signaling

Recommendation: do NOT put "part-time" on /hire or the resumes. Keep the public
position full-time. Offer fractional/contract-to-hire selectively in conversation
as a de-risking close ("try me for 90 days") — it converts the thin employment
history into an easy yes instead of reading as low commitment.
